Using External Videos (X / Instagram)

GameFrame does not download videos directly from social media. You bring the video in yourself.

This is intentional. It keeps uploads fast, avoids broken imports, and works well because most real baseball clips already live on X (Twitter) and Instagram.

How to do it

  1. Find the clip
    Open the post on X or Instagram that contains the pitch, swing, bullpen rep, or at-bat.
  2. Copy the link
    Use Share → Copy link.
  3. Download the video
    For X (Twitter):
    https://twittervideodownloader.com/download

    For Instagram:
    https://snapinsta.to

    Paste the post link and download the MP4.
  4. Choose the best quality
    Pick the highest-resolution MP4 available.
  5. Upload into GameFrame
    Upload the MP4 as a Pitch or Swing clip like any other video.

Good to know

  • X and Instagram clips are usually short, which is ideal for analysis.
  • Desktop and Android work best; on iOS, files may land in the Files app.
  • Private or restricted posts cannot be downloaded.
  • If a post has multiple videos, make sure you grab the correct one.

Why GameFrame works this way

Social platforms change constantly. By keeping downloading outside the app, GameFrame avoids broken imports, codec problems, and slow processing.

You control the video. GameFrame stays focused on frame-accurate baseball analysis.
