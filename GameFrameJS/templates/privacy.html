Privacy & Data

Your baseball library stays local. Teams, player names, uploaded videos, tags, pose data, generated matchups, collages, and dashboard history are stored in the GameFrame database on this computer.

GameFrame's online service receives the account email, a one-way device identifier, app version, license status, matchup authorization and completion events, processing time, and limited failure codes. GameFrame does not need to upload the local baseball video library to authorize usage.

Users control their local backups and are responsible for protecting copies that contain player footage. Obtain appropriate consent before recording or processing minors.

Pre-release notice: This disclosure describes the current technical design and should receive legal review before public sale.
