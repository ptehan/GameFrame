Usage Terms

GameFrame is a video timing and coaching aid. Its measurements depend on video frame rate, camera position, and the accuracy of tagged frames; they are not guarantees of athletic performance.

Users must own or have permission to record, download, upload, process, and share footage used with GameFrame. Public availability on a website does not by itself grant reuse rights.

Users are responsible for obtaining consent for player footage, particularly footage involving minors, and for complying with applicable team, school, league, platform, and copyright requirements.
