Swing Trim Help
  1. Use Autodetect to suggest frames, or set them manually.
  2. For autodetect: click Autodetect, then click the hitter in the preview.
  3. Choose the contact frame that looks right.
  4. Manual Start: first move (front foot lift, or first lateral hip move in a no-stride swing).
  5. Manual Decision: when the hands launch.
  6. Manual Contact: where the bat would hit the ball.
  7. Finalize when Start, Decision, and Contact look correct.
